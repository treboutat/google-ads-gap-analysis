# Sources & basis

This skill is the public version of TNT Growth's internal gap-analysis workflow, aligned with our approved Google Ads method (release `2026-10-14.1`). Notes on its claims so they're defensible.

## What a GAP keyword is
A search term from the analysis window that fits a winning theme, isn't already an enabled keyword in a live ad group and campaign, and can actually serve (no current negative blocks it). Each one needs a purpose: routing control, ad or page relevance for a distinct need, or reach the account doesn't get today. "It converted" alone isn't a purpose.

## Judged on mature outcomes, the way the campaign bids
Winners are defined by each campaign's actual bid strategy and the business's acceptable economics, read from clicks old enough for the conversion lag. When economics aren't stated, the observed non-brand CPA or ROAS is used as a labelled baseline, which describes the account and doesn't prove profitability.

## No universal thresholds
There's no click, spend or day cutoff for adding or excluding a term, and no minimum search volume. Clear mismatches can be excluded at zero clicks; relevant terms with thin data stay eligible on a watch list with an exposure limit.

## Lift is a range, not a promise
The lift estimate is quarterly value at stake times editable low and high incremental shares. It's platform conversion value, not revenue. A later before/after comparison is observational and doesn't prove incrementality.

## Platform facts
Statements about how Google Ads behaves (negative matching, close variants, report coverage, pinning, PMax and AI Max reporting) link to Google's documentation and were checked in October 2026. Google changes the platform often; re-verify before a recommendation depends on one. The negative-matching script models Google's documented rules but can't model the misspellings Google says negatives also catch, so a clean result is necessary, not sufficient.

## What this skill deliberately does not claim
- No client names or client results. Worked examples and test fixtures are fictional.
- No savings figures: spend on proposed negatives is shown as historical spend in the window.
- No CPA-reduction projection, forecast or guaranteed lift.
- Recommendations aren't account changes. It makes none.
