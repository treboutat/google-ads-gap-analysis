# Choosing assets for GAP ad groups

Sources: Asset performance report (Search rows, same quarter) + 90-day ad-level pull for the target campaigns (headlines, descriptions, final URL, metrics per ad).

Ranking
- Max Conversions / tCPA: cost per conversion vs the effective target or acceptable cost (the labelled non-brand baseline when neither is known), with value per conversion as the tie-breaker (a $1-value conversion does not prove an asset). Use the mature window.
- Value bidding / tROAS: conversion value and ROAS with enough volume.
- Asset rows describe assets serving together. They are diagnostic, not isolated effects. High "Used by" counts usually include Brand ads and look better than they would in non-brand. Prefer assets proven inside the same intent (competitor-ad assets for competitor groups, Image assets for an Image group), then add the best general assets.

What to reuse, what to drop
- Reuse proven assets verbatim. Changing even capitalization or punctuation creates a new asset without history; note it if you do.
- Leave out vague CTAs, competitor puns that didn't convert and unsubstantiated numeric claims. Deprioritize assets with meaningful mature spend and no or $1-value conversions; because asset rows are not isolated effects, say why in "Existing assets deliberately left out" rather than calling them losers.
- New or derived assets may fill a 15/4 set or a missing angle (e.g. a pricing message built from a top sitelink's lines). Label each one "New / derived" with its source.

Must-checks
- CTA matches the final URL ("Request a Quote" → pricing-request page; "Schedule A Demo" → demo page).
- Competitor ads: advertiser identity clear; no competitor names in headlines, descriptions or paths; no keyword insertion in competitor/trademark groups.
- Keyword insertion: fallback text ≤30 chars; longer matched keywords fall back to it.
- Claims: check each objective claim against the landing page or another source and flag conflicting or stale claims (customer counts, awards, ROI stats) for the business to confirm. Keep required qualifiers (price floors, eligibility, insurance limits) and regulated-category rules.
- Final URL: use ad-level evidence (e.g. the same assets converting far better on a pricing-request URL than a demo URL).
- Unpinned unless a required message (disclosure, brand requirement, truthful qualifier) needs a pin; name what the pin protects. Final URL expansion and campaign text disclaimers can defeat a pin; check both.
- Ad group stays at ≤3 enabled RSAs; if unknown, add a QA CHECK.
